TECHNICAL SUPPORT POLICY

For failed payments, support should first verify whether
the payment provider accepted or rejected the transaction.

Customers should not be charged twice for the same order.

If money was deducted but the order remains unpaid,
support should verify the transaction status before asking
the customer to retry payment.

If the payment status cannot be confirmed, create a support
ticket and escalate the issue.

Never ask customers to provide passwords or full payment
card numbers.
