REFUND POLICY

Customers may request a refund within 30 days
of the original purchase date.

Refund requests must include the order number.

Refunds are normally processed within 5 business days
after approval.

Premium customers may receive refunds for eligible
annual subscriptions according to their contract terms.

Digital products that have been fully consumed may not
be eligible for a refund.

If a customer believes a refund decision is incorrect,
the case should be escalated to a human support specialist.
